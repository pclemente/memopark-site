How it works

From parking to walking back.

  1. 01 /

    Save the spot

    Tap to save your position. If the garage has a weak GPS signal, save a pin at the entrance while you have a fix.

  2. 02 /

    Remember the details

    Take a photo of the floor sign or add a note like “B2, row C, by the elevators.” Your map pin is only part of the story.

  3. 03 /

    Walk back

    Reopen the saved pin and check your photo or note. Open walking directions in Apple Maps or, if installed, Google Maps.
